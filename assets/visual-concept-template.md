## Hero moment

The one instant the knowledge becomes visible. Describe it concretely
(objects, motion, what appears/changes) and where it lands on the timeline
(should be reachable early — not parked at 95% of a 30 s story).

## Story in one sentence

"A ___ travels/grows/snaps ___, and the learner watches ___ happen."

## Attention flow

How the eye is steered between beats: zoom-ins, glow hand-offs, dimming of
the non-focal, motion contrast (what stops when what moves).

## Mood / palette / stage

colors reused, typography notes, vignette/lighting choices.

## Interaction promise

What the learner can click, drag, change, or switch (inspection, exploded
view, parameters, scenarios) — and what each interaction teaches.

## Level justification

claim is what the visual quality gate will score.
